# Morpheus brand asset

The approved original is `src/assets/morpheus-gradient-logo.png`; its transparent cutout is `src/assets/morpheus-gradient-logo-transparent.png`. The user approved the gradient concept on 1 November 2026. Both images were generated using the built-in image tool; no API key or external paid job was used. The original raster is preserved unchanged. `src/brand.tsx` displays the original colours through the cutout's alpha mask; dark headers render the wordmark in white for contrast. The README and browser icon use the transparent cutout directly.

The symbol uses folded ribbons and an open portal to suggest movement between physical and simulated space. The palette moves from turquoise through blue and violet with a coral highlight. The exact wordmark is `morpheus`.

Generation prompt:

> Generate a completely fresh, original logo concept for MORPHEUS, a product that transforms a scan of your home into a simulated world for household robots. Create a memorable fluid M symbol made from two elegantly folded continuous ribbons, with a subtle open portal in its negative space. The form should suggest moving between real and simulated space, with simple beautiful geometry and a compact silhouette usable as an app icon. Use rich, expertly blended gradient colours flowing from luminous turquoise through electric blue into violet, with a restrained warm coral highlight at a fold. Pair it with a custom lowercase wordmark spelling exactly 'morpheus', in near-black ink, soft geometric typography with meticulous letter spacing. Single horizontal lockup on a light warm-white background. No cubes, house clipart, robot head, brain, infinity symbol, pill, stock swoosh, mockup, tagline or watermark.
